> *"A union of gods and mortals under the radiant banners of Solareth and Namarielle."*

The **Alliance of Life** was the great union of gods and mortals forged in desperation during the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]] — the last time the Firstborn walked openly beside the peoples of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]].

## The hosts

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Solareth\|Solareth]]** raised the banners of the living; **[[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Namarielle\|Namarielle]]** stood at the heart of its healing and renewal.
- The **[[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Elves\|Elves]]** of Vael'Tharion, guided by the direct voice of [[rosvania/Wiki/Vael'Thara\|Vael'Thara]], became the Alliance's spellweavers and healers.
- The **[[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Dwarves\|Dwarves]]** of [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Durakheim\|Durakheim]], under [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Korran-Dur\|Korran-Dur]], forged **[[rosvania/Wiki/Metals of Rosvania\|light-forged steel]]** — the only metal that could wound [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]'s kin, the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/The Unmade\|The Unmade]].
- The **[[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Men\|Men]]** of Tuzland sent a small host, but their greatest champions — the Demigods of the Hammered Sun, children of [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Solareth\|Solareth]]'s blessing, led by Tuzlak the Great King and the mighty Big G — fought at the fore.

## The victory

In the year 10 FA, the combined hosts stormed the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Black Spire of Mourning\|Black Spire of Mourning]]. Solareth struck the final blow, shattering Vharuk's body; [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Athera\|Athera]] and Namarielle sealed his soul beneath the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]].

## Aftermath

The Alliance's work done, the Wardens withdrew and the world passed into mortal hands. Its legacy is the proof that life, united, can stand against the silence — a lesson the [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Third Age\|Third Age]] may need again.
