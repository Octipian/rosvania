> *"The Frost-Reavers, pale marauders of the ice, now sail their black ships upon the storm-torn seas."*

The **Frost-Reavers** are the pale marauders of the farthest north — sea-thieves who sail black ships upon the storm-torn seas at the edge of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Doomlands\|Doomlands]].

## Nature

The Frost-Reavers raid out of the ice-smitten waters where the Breath of Vharuk meets the sea. They are the first wave of the north's terror made flesh — naval raiders the length of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]]'s coasts have learned to dread.

## The Third Age

With the breaking of the First Seal, the Reavers' raids press ever farther south. They clash with the [[rosvania/Wiki/Factions & Organizations/Tuzlean Corsairs\|Tuzlean Corsairs]] upon the southern seas, their black sails meeting black sails while the courts of Men watch only the landward wars. Among the whispers of the north, some say the Reavers are not merely raiders but heralds — the outriders of the stirring [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/The Unmade\|host beneath the ice]].
