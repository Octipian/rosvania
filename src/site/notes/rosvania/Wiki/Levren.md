> *"From the shadows watched Levren, whose allegiance none yet knew."*

**Levren** is the quietest and least known of the Chosen Party — a figure who watched from the shadows before ever entering the light, and whose allegiance none yet know.

## The watcher

At the goblin ambush on the road to [[rosvania/Wiki/Arglaxx\|Arglaxx]], Levren watched from the shadows as the battle unfolded — the fourth figure of the age, drawn into [[rosvania/Wiki/Golgoth\|Golgoth]]'s quest by fate, curiosity, or the promise of redemption.

## Cautious and cursed

- In [[rosvania/Wiki/Lotusden\|Lotusden]], where the blacksmith scammed both [[rosvania/Wiki/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Gerard\|Gerard]], Levren — more cautious — secured a single weapon worth the gold.
- From the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deck of Some Things\|Deck of Some Things]], Levren's hand summoned a **vengeful paladin fated to hunt him** — a curse that followed the party to the [[rosvania/Wiki/Swamp of Sh'rek\|Swamp of Sh'rek]], where its paladin appeared from the reeds before a colossal hand of black tentacles dragged it screaming beneath the surface.
