**Khar'doth the Uniter** is the most legendary warlord in Orc history — the one who united the tribes of [[rosvania/Wiki/Gorthul-Maar\|Gorthul-Maar]] and carved an empire from the wreckage of the [[rosvania/Wiki/First Age\|First Age]].

## The rise

In the chaos that followed the [[rosvania/Wiki/Shattered Sea War\|Shattered Sea War]] — as [[rosvania/Wiki/Erynos\|Erynos]] drowned [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Menor]] and the world's power lay leaderless — Khar'doth rallied the scattered Orc tribes under a single banner for the first time since the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]].

## The conquest

Leading a great horde south from Gorthul-Maar, Khar'doth conquered the leaderless eastern lands. So ended the First Age — in salt, ruin, silence, and the triumphant war-cries of his Orcs.

## Legacy

- Under Khar'doth, the Orcs ruled the eastern plains through the Years of Ash — a golden age of dominance that ended only when the [[rosvania/Wiki/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]] rose and pushed the hordes back north.
- His name remains the standard by which all Orc warlords are measured — including the present Warlord [[rosvania/Wiki/Warlord Gorgath\|Gorgath]].
