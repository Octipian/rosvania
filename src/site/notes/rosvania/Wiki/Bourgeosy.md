**Bourgeosy** is a bustling city of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] where the Chosen Party paused in the first year of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]] to seek counsel on the [[rosvania/Wiki/Cursed Map\|Cursed Map]].

## The vanished mage

A learned mage of Bourgeosy agreed to study the map. When he peered into its depths, horror seized him. Without a word, he vanished that very night — fleeing, perhaps, to warn others, or perhaps to hide from what he had seen. He would return, years later and hollow-eyed, in [[rosvania/Wiki/Zaporizzia\|Zaporizzia]].
