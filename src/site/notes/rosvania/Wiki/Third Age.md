The **Third Age** is the present age of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] — the **Age of Shadow and Return**. It began when the First Seal of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]] cracked, ending the Still Years with a whisper like ice fracturing across eternity. The age of waiting was done.

## The Breaking of the Seal

None knew when the seal had broken — only that the stars burned dimmer and the northern winds carried death. The great silence was broken, and the breath of [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]] began to seep into the living world.

## The State of the World

- **The far north:** the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]] spew the Breath of Vharuk — an unending, devouring blizzard. The [[rosvania/Wiki/Frost-Reavers\|Frost-Reavers]] sail black ships; the [[rosvania/Wiki/The Unmade\|The Unmade]] stir in legions beneath an aurora-less sky.
- **The center:** the [[rosvania/Wiki/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]] stands proud but decaying under [[rosvania/Wiki/Emperor Taryon V\|Emperor Taryon V]], who calls the omens superstition — guided toward blindness by the unseen counsel of [[rosvania/Wiki/Vorun Khar\|Vorun Khar]].
- **The southwest:** the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Corsairs\|Tuzlean Corsairs]] rule the coasts; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Hold of Five Trials\|Hold of Five Trials]] watches the encroaching dark with wary resolve.
- **The northwest:** in [[rosvania/Wiki/Etharion\|Etharion]], the Eldertree trembles and the Earth-Song turns to dirge; the [[rosvania/Wiki/High Synod\|High Synod]] debates whether to walk beside mortals again.
- **The mountains:** [[rosvania/Wiki/Durakheim\|Durakheim]] is sealed; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] forge weapons of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Metals of Rosvania\|Deep's Gift]] in grim preparation.
- **The east:** the [[rosvania/Wiki/Silver Accord\|Silver Accord]] lands remain bright with trade, their prophets speaking of shadows beneath the sea.

## Year 1–3 TA

- **Year 1:** the Chosen Party gathers on the road to [[rosvania/Wiki/Arglaxx\|Arglaxx]], receives a task from [[rosvania/Wiki/Golgoth\|Golgoth]], the last of the elder drakes, and survives the [[rosvania/Wiki/Cursed Map\|Trial of Will]] with the cursed map. (See [[rosvania/Wiki/Session 1 - The Chosen Party\|Session 1 - The Chosen Party]].)
- **Year 2:** [[rosvania/Wiki/Floris Flex\|Floris Flex]] departs; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]] rises in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Swamp of Sh'rek\|Swamp of Sh'rek]]; a hollow-eyed wizard proclaims: *"Vharuk wakes again."* (See [[rosvania/Wiki/Session 2 - The Breaking of Fellowship\|Session 2 - The Breaking of Fellowship]].)
- **Year 3:** the party sails the [[rosvania/Wiki/Good ol' Jacky\|Good ol' Jacky]], survives [[rosvania/Wiki/Captain Serak\|Captain Serak]] and the wrath of [[rosvania/Wiki/Erynos\|Erynos]], and wins the Trial of Combat before the [[rosvania/Wiki/Protectors of the Realm\|Protectors of the Realm]]. (See [[rosvania/Wiki/Session 3 - The Trial of Combat\|Session 3 - The Trial of Combat]].)

The winds from the north howl once more. The new age has not only begun — it is accelerating.
