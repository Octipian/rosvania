> # Black Spire of Mourning
> **Type** — Fortress (ruin)

> *"The combined hosts stormed the Black Spire of Mourning in the far north."*

The **Black Spire of Mourning** is the fortress of the far north where the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]] reached its end — the stronghold of [[Vharuk\|Vharuk]] the Stillborn Flame, and the place where his body was shattered.

## The final battle

In the year 10 FA, the combined hosts of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Alliance of Life\|Alliance of Life]] — [[rosvania/Wiki/Elves\|Elves]] spellweavers, [[rosvania/Wiki/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] with light-forged steel, and the champions of Men — stormed the Black Spire. There, **[[rosvania/Wiki/Solareth\|Solareth]] struck the final blow**, shattering Vharuk's body. His soul could not be slain; [[rosvania/Wiki/Athera\|Athera]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Namarielle\|Namarielle]] bound it beneath the world.

## Today

The Spire stands in the creeping shadow of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]], a monument to the victory that bought the world its long ages of peace — and a watch-point for the day the prisoned god wakes again.
