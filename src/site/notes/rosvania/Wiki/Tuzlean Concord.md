> *"A brotherhood of warlords who forged unity through necessity."*

The **Tuzlean Concord** was the brotherhood of human warlords who, in the dark of the Years of Ash, forged unity through necessity and raised the southwest of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] from ruin.

## Founding and rule

In 313–689 SA, from the chaos of the Years of Ash, the lords of the south-west bound themselves into the Concord. It was a league of pirates' sons and ruined kings — and it held. Under the Concord's peace, the land breathed again.

## End

With the return of prosperity, ambition followed. The noble houses of the Concord turned on one another, and the league was consumed by the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Civil War\|Tuzlean Civil War]] — a war of nearly three centuries that left Tuzland in salt and division.

## Legacy

- The ground on which the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Corsairs\|Tuzlean Corsairs]] and the [[rosvania/Wiki/Hold of Five Trials\|Hold of Five Trials]] were later built.
- Proof that even divided peoples can unite against ruin — a lesson of the Forgeborn age the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]] may yet recall.
