**Namarielle** is the **Lifebringer**, Mother of Renewal, and the Warden of all living growth. She is the second of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Firstborn Wardens\|Firstborn Wardens]] in honor, and the goddess most wronged by [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]'s fall.

## Nature

Namarielle is the breath of spring, the turning of the seasons, and the patient renewal of the world. Her domain is growth itself — the [[rosvania/Wiki/Elves\|Elves]] of [[rosvania/Wiki/Etharion\|Etharion]] hear her remaining song in the roots of the Eldertree.

## The Stolen Essence

Before the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]], [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]] stole from Namarielle the very breath of renewal and corrupted it — birthing the first [[rosvania/Wiki/The Unmade\|The Unmade]], beings neither living nor dead, perfect in their hollow stillness. This theft, more than anything, made the betrayal unforgivable.

## History

- In the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]], Namarielle fought beside [[rosvania/Wiki/Solareth\|Solareth]] in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Alliance of Life\|Alliance of Life]] as healer and renewer.
- At the war's end, she joined [[rosvania/Wiki/Athera\|Athera]] in binding [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]'s soul beneath the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]] — a prison of eternal ice and silence.
- In the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]], the rise of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]] — a mockery of life "profound" enough to shatter the [[rosvania/Wiki/High Synod\|High Synod]]'s centuries of debate — is seen as a perversion of her very domain.

## Legacy

- The [[rosvania/Wiki/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] say the [[rosvania/Wiki/Metals of Rosvania\|Deep's Gift]] resists the chill of Vharuk's Breath; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Elves\|Elves]] say her voice still weeps under the world.
- The [[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]]'s rebirth from the mists of the old war is a wound upon her domain that all the living feel.
