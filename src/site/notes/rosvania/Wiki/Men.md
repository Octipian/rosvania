> *"Men from the soil between them."*

**Men** are the youngest of the elder races — born from the soil of the world by the Third Decree. They are the builders of empires, the makers of wars, and the race most changed by the long ages.

## Nature

Men are the race of the middle: not long-lived as the Elves nor unyielding as the Dwarves, but endless in ambition and renewal. Their history is a rhythm of rise, pride, fall, and rise again.

## History

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]]:** Tuzland sent a small host to the [[rosvania/Wiki/Alliance of Life\|Alliance of Life]] — but its champions, the Demigods of the Hammered Sun, were children of [[rosvania/Wiki/Solareth\|Solareth]]'s blessing.
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/First Age\|First Age]]:** the first human empire, [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Menor]], arose and fell in pride; Tuzland was half-drowned by [[rosvania/Wiki/Erynos\|Erynos]].
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Second Age\|Second Age]]:** the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Concord\|Tuzlean Concord]] and then the [[rosvania/Wiki/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]] rebuilt the world of Men; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Civil War\|Tuzlean Civil War]] divided the west; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Corsairs\|Tuzlean Corsairs]] took the coasts.
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]]:** the Dominion stands at the center — caught between the very real Orc war in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Silver Marches\|Silver Marches]] and the hidden doom of the north, its emperor blind to the whisper in [[rosvania/Wiki/Velmoria\|Velmoria]]'s halls.
