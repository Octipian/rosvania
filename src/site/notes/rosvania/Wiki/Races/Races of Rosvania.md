# Races of Rosvania

> *"By the Third Decree, He breathed upon the clay of the world, and life took root."*

An index of the peoples of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] — the elder races of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Unseen Father\|Father's]] design, the forged races of [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]], and the smaller kindreds of the world.

## The elder races

Created by the Third Decree, formed in the image of the Father:

- [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Elves\|Elves]] — from starlight; the eldest and fairest, born under [[rosvania/Wiki/Vael'Thara\|Vael'Thara]]'s song.
- [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] — from mountain-heart; the children of [[rosvania/Wiki/Korran-Dur\|Korran-Dur]], lords of stone and forge.
- [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Men\|Men]] — from the soil between; builders, kings, and wanderers, greatest in number and in change.

## The forged races

Twisted by [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]] in secret, before the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]]:

- [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Orcs\|Orcs]] — from the great wolves and serpents of the mountains; brutal, and consumed by a thirst for dominance.
- [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Goblins\|Goblins]] — from the crawling things of the deep earth; petty, greedy, and thriving on the ruin of others.

## The lesser kindreds

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Halflings\|Halflings]]** — warm-hearted folk of hearth and harvest, found in towns like [[rosvania/Wiki/Bumblebrook\|Bumblebrook]].
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Tieflings\|Tieflings]]** — children of crimson lanterns and thieves' honor, found in cities like [[rosvania/Wiki/Lotusden\|Lotusden]].
- **Seafolk** — the salt-dripping servants of [[rosvania/Wiki/Captain Serak\|Captain Serak]]; the lore records them dripping with seawater and malice.
- **Elder drakes** — the lineage of [[rosvania/Wiki/Golgoth\|Golgoth]], last of his kind.

## The unmade

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/The Unmade\|The Unmade]]** — not a people but a corruption; beings neither living nor dead, forged from [[rosvania/Wiki/Namarielle\|Namarielle]]'s stolen essence.
