> *"A race of petty, greedy creatures who would thrive on the misery of others."*

The **Goblins** are the second of [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]'s forged races — mean and covetous, made from the crawling things of the deep earth, and prosperous only in the ruin of others.

## Origin and exile

Forged by Vharuk before the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]], the Goblins infested the southern [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Emberwastes\|Emberwastes]] after his fall, preying on the weak. In the [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/First Age\|First Age]], the legions of the [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Solar Empire of Menor]] drove them from their ancestral home — the expulsion that taught them the true shape of power.

## The Syndicates

In the forests of [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Vael'nore\|Vael'nore]], the Goblins abandoned open warfare for an insidious enterprise: slavery. Under figures like **Grak the Chain-Master**, they formed the first [[rosvania/Wiki/Factions & Organizations/Goblin Syndicates\|Goblin Syndicates]]. In the [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Second Age\|Second Age]], the lanista **Batrok** grew rich providing gladiators for the arenas of the continent.

## The Third Age

The Syndicates flourish beneath the bright markets of the east — a quiet rot the courts of Men ignore while the Orc war consumes their attention.
