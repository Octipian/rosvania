> *"The Elves from starlight."*

The **Elves** are the eldest of the elder races — born from starlight by the Third Decree of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Unseen Father\|Unseen Father]], and shaped into grace by the song of [[rosvania/Wiki/Vael'Thara\|Vael'Thara]].

## Nature

The Elves are the dreamers of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]]: long-lived, fair, and bound to the song of the world. They hear the **Earth-Song** — the voice of the land itself — a gift no other race possesses.

## History

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]]:** the Elves of Vael'Tharion, guided by the direct voice of Vael'Thara, were the [[rosvania/Wiki/Alliance of Life\|Alliance of Life]]'s spellweavers and healers.
- **The [[rosvania/Wiki/First Age\|First Age]]:** the [[rosvania/Wiki/Whisperer Beneath the Roots\|Whisperer Beneath the Roots]] seduced Queen [[rosvania/Wiki/Vael'Nir\|Vael'Nir]], and Etharion became the Radiant Dominion. In the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of the Radiant Blades\|War of the Radiant Blades]], the Elves marched in holy fire against Men — and, when the lie was burned away in grief, they renounced all divine worship, shamed by their folly.
- **The ages between:** the Elves hid in the deep forests, governed by the [[rosvania/Wiki/High Synod\|High Synod]] in endless twilight, guarding the roots of their realm.

## The Third Age

The Eldertree shrieks, the Earth-Song twists into a sorrowful dirge, and the rise of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]] has shattered the Synod's debate. The time for isolation is over: the Elves must decide whether to walk beside mortals once more.
