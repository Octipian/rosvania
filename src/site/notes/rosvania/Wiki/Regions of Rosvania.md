# Regions of Rosvania

> *"The currents of destiny stir once more beneath the cold breath of the north."*

An index of the known lands of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]], from the frozen [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]] to the burning [[rosvania/Wiki/Emberwastes\|Emberwastes]].

## The Far North

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]]** — the frozen prison of [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]; origin of the Breath of Vharuk, the devouring blizzard of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]].
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Black Spire of Mourning\|Black Spire of Mourning]]** — the fallen fortress in the far north where [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]] was defeated.
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Durakheim\|Durakheim]]** — the north-central mountains; sealed halls of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Dwarves\|Dwarves]].

## The Northwest

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Etharion\|Etharion]]** — the deep forests of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Elves\|Elves]]; home of the Eldertree and the [[rosvania/Wiki/High Synod\|High Synod]].

## The Center

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]]** — the golden empire of Men, ruled from [[rosvania/Wiki/Velmoria\|Velmoria]] by [[rosvania/Wiki/Emperor Taryon V\|Emperor Taryon V]].
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Velmoria\|Velmoria]]** — capital of the Dominion, with a whisper of hidden counsel in its halls.

## The Southwest

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzland and the Whispering Sands\|Tuzland and the Whispering Sands]]** — the shattered remnants of old empires; corsairs' coasts and sunken fields.
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Hold of Five Trials\|Hold of Five Trials]]** — the highland republic bound by oath, and the trials of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Protectors of the Realm\|Protectors of the Realm]].

## The East

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Silver Marches\|Silver Marches]]** — the northeastern plains; border of the Orc raids, walled against [[rosvania/Wiki/Gorthul-Maar\|Gorthul-Maar]].
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Gorthul-Maar\|Gorthul-Maar]]** — the black plains, brutal homeland of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Orcs\|Orcs]].
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Vael'nore\|Vael'nore]]** — southeastern forests and trade cities of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Silver Accord\|Silver Accord]]; lair of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Goblin Syndicates\|Goblin Syndicates]].

## The South

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Emberwastes\|Emberwastes]]** — the burning woods, old Goblin homeland, now infested with Syndicate slave-pens.

## The Wetlands

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Zaporizzia\|Zaporizzia]]** — Kazzimir's swampy homeland.
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Swamp of Sh'rek\|Swamp of Sh'rek]]** — the plague-ridden mire, cradle of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]].

## Notable Cities & Sites

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Arglaxx\|Arglaxx]]** — the city of dust and deceit of the First Session.
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Bourgeosy\|Bourgeosy]]** — the bustling city where the mage fled the [[rosvania/Wiki/Cursed Map\|Cursed Map]].
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Bumblebrook\|Bumblebrook]]** — the halfling town of warm hearths and foolish hearts.
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Lotusden\|Lotusden]]** — the tiefling city of crimson lanterns and thieves' honor.
