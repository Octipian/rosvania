**Vorun Khar** is the Hidden Witness — [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]'s right hand, a mortal fragment of the fallen god's will, who walks the courts of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] in the guise of a friend.

## Nature

Vorun Khar is not a god, but a fragment of one — a mortal echo of the Stillborn Flame, patient and wise, wearing countless names. The sorcerers of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Factions & Organizations/Silver Accord\|Silver Accord]] call him the **Hidden Witness**: a spy sent to measure mortal hearts before Desolation awakens.

## Methods

- A friend to kings, a wanderer among scholars, a voice of reason — he bends the great to blindness without ever being seen to bend.
- He uses the very real Orc threat of Warlord [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Warlord Gorgath\|Gorgath]] to distract [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Emperor Taryon V\|Emperor Taryon V]], ensuring the world remains blind to the true darkness stirring in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Doomlands\|Doomlands]].
- In [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Velmoria\|Velmoria]], his is the "quiet influence" that seeps through the halls — "a whisper of counsel none can trace."
- Some theologians whisper that his freedom to walk unwatched is itself a veil of [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Serathis\|Serathis]] — for concealment does not discriminate about who it protects.

## The Third Age

None have seen him. None can prove his existence. But as the seals falter and the Doomlands stir, the wise tremble — for *"even false light can look like hope, and in these Still Years, the world holds its breath before the dawn of Desolation."*
