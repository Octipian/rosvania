**Rizzhia** was a marsh kingdom of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Second Age\|Second Age]] — a realm built on fish, reed-cloth, and amber that stood where the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Swamp of Sh'rek\|Swamp of Sh'rek]] lies today, until plague undid besiegers and besieged alike, and something old beneath the peat woke in the graves.

## Founding (c. 40–60 SA)

Refugees fleeing the collapse of [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Menor]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Tuzland and the Whispering Sands\|Tuzland]] settled the marsh, founding Rizzhia under the line of Sh'rek. The ground was not unclaimed: in the Age Before Ages it had been sacred ground to [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Athera\|Athera]] — patron of wild beasts, moonlight, and the border between hunter and hunted, a grove or pact-stone of hers said to stand in the deep mire. By Rizzhia's day her power was just half-forgotten superstition among the marsh-folk, left undisturbed beneath the peat.

## The Marsh Kingdom (c. 60–290 SA)

Rizzhia thrived modestly for over two centuries. Its economy was built on fish, reed-cloth, and amber traded upriver to its neighbors — a small, steady realm that made no great noise in the histories of the age.

## The Siege (c. 290 SA)

A lesser Orc chieftain — not [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Khar'doth the Uniter\|Khar'doth the Uniter]] — laid siege to the kingdom. The siege dragged on long enough that plague broke out in both camps. Rizzhia fell not to the sword but to sickness; when it was over, besiegers and besieged alike ended up in the same mass graves.

## The King Wakes (c. 290 SA)

Something already buried in that ground — a splinter of [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]'s [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/The Unmade\|The Unmade]], dormant since the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]] — stirred to life inside the mass grave and rose as the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]], a will built from every corpse in the marsh. Rizzhia's ruins became the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Swamp of Sh'rek\|Swamp of Sh'rek]].

## Legacy

- **A footnote of the Years of Ash:** Rizzhia rose (c. 40–60 SA) and fell (c. 290 SA) entirely within the dark first century of the ash — arguably the **first Second Age nation to rise and fall**. It never got the chance to be part of the recovery; it was already a ghost by the time recovery began.
- The line of Sh'rek gave the swamp its name — the Swamp of Sh'rek.
- The **Rizzhian War(s)**: not one war, but a recurring one. Whenever the King's rot grows strong enough to press outward, neighboring realms muster to push it back; each flare-up is remembered locally as "the Rizzhian War," though they are centuries apart and fought by different peoples each time.
- The death of [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] in the deep mire (c. 1495 SA) — and his rebirth as a Dhampir — in ground still faintly claimed by Athera's forgotten power. The King, by every account, "lost" a body that should have been his.
