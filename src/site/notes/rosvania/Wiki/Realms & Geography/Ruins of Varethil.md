**Ruins of Varethil** — once the mining city of **Varethil** — is a burned and abandoned town at the foot of [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Ashrend Peaks\|Ashrend Peak]], destroyed by dragonfire in the late [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Second Age\|Second Age]] and never rebuilt. Its charred streets and collapsed tunnels are all that remain of the sunmetal city.

## Founding (1240 SA)

Cassian Vareth, a prospector-turned-minor noble granted a land claim by the Dominion of Aurelian for ore rights, set up a mining camp at the foot of Ashrend Peak after a vein of iron — and a rarer fire-touched gold the locals call **sunmetal** — was found in the rock. (See [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Metals of Rosvania#Sunmetal\|Sunmetal]].)

## City charter (1245 SA)

The camp grew fast enough — sunmetal drew prospectors, and the peak sat on the southern trade road toward the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Hold of Five Trials\|Hold of Five Trials]] — that [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Velmoria\|Velmoria]] formally chartered it as a city, named for its founder. It answered to the Dominion of Aurelian but was left largely self-governing.

## Growth (1245–1400 SA)

Varethil became a proper crossroads town: humans, elves, a handful of dwarven smiths, and traders passing to and from the Trial-lands settled there. Population climbed past four thousand, and the sunmetal trade made it modestly wealthy. Ashrend Peak became a known but unremarkable landmark — old tales spoke of *something ancient* in the high caves, never confirmed in living memory.

## Slow decline (c. 1400–1480 SA)

The richest sunmetal veins ran dry. Varethil did not collapse, but it stopped growing — settling into a smaller trade and mining town, propped up by the road traffic more than the mines.

## The Burning (1487 SA)

A dragon nesting on Ashrend Peak to give birth stirred the city to arms. Varethil's militia — and much of the adult population, including [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Levren\|Levren Thorne]]'s parents, Cedric Thorne and Serenya Larethiel — marched up the slopes and drove the dragon off. Wounded and enraged, it returned over the city on its way out. Fire spread fast through the close-packed wooden districts, and the burning support timbers beneath the ore-tunnels collapsed, taking parts of the city down with them. Roughly a third of the population survived, scattering into the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Emberwastes\|Emberwastes]] and the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Hold of Five Trials\|Hold of Five Trials]].

## Aftermath (1487 SA onward)

The city was never rebuilt. The tapped-out mines gave nobody reason to resettle under the mountain, and the fear of the dragon's return did the rest. It appears on the current map simply as the **Ruins of Varethil**.
