The **Swamp of Sh'rek** is a plague-haunted mire of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] and the hidden cradle of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]] — a place where the dead of an old war never truly rested.

## History

| Years | Event |
| --- | --- |
| Age Before Ages | The marsh is sacred ground to [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Athera\|Athera]] — patron of wild beasts, moonlight, and the border between hunter and hunted. A grove or pact-stone of hers is said to have stood somewhere in the deep mire. |
| c. 40–60 SA | **The founding of [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Rizzhia\|Rizzhia]].** Refugees fleeing the collapse of [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Menor]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Tuzland and the Whispering Sands\|Tuzland]] settle the marsh, founding the kingdom of Rizzhia under the line of Sh'rek. Athera's old power, by then just half-forgotten superstition among the marsh-folk, is left undisturbed beneath the peat. |
| c. 60–290 SA | **The Marsh Kingdom.** Rizzhia thrives modestly for over two centuries, its economy built on fish, reed-cloth, and amber traded upriver to its neighbors. |
| c. 290 SA | **The Siege.** A lesser Orc chieftain (not [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Khar'doth the Uniter\|Khar'doth]]) lays siege to Rizzhia. The siege drags on long enough that plague breaks out in both camps. Rizzhia falls not to the sword but to sickness — besiegers and besieged alike end up in the same mass graves when it is over. |
| c. 290 SA | **The King Wakes.** A splinter of [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]'s [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/The Unmade\|The Unmade]], dormant since the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]], stirs to life inside the mass grave and rises as the Necrotic King — a will built from every corpse in the marsh. Rizzhia's ruins become the Swamp of Sh'rek. |
| 290 SA onward | **The Rizzhian War(s).** Not one war but a recurring one: whenever the King's rot grows strong enough to press outward, neighboring realms muster to push it back. Each flare-up gets remembered locally as "the Rizzhian War," though they are centuries apart and fought by different peoples each time. |
| c. 1470s SA | [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] grows up in [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Zaporizzia\|Zaporizzia]], on the swamp's edge, and takes up work as a mercenary — the kind of man who sells death for coin, with no particular loyalty to any of the realms that keep fighting over the mire. |
| c. 1495 SA | **A flare-up, and a death.** The King's legion pushes outward again — a smaller, localized surge rather than a kingdom-scale war. Kazzimir is hired into the fighting and dies in the deep mire, in ground still faintly claimed by Athera's forgotten power. |
| c. 1495 SA | **The Swamp's Second Breath.** Rather than rising as one of the King's mindless dead, Kazzimir wakes into something caught between two powers — Athera's wildness and the King's rot — and becomes a **Dhampir**: neither the King's servant nor fully alive. The King, by every account, "lost" a body that should have been his. |
| c. 1495–1504 SA | **Kazzimir of the Mire.** For roughly nine years, Kazzimir all but vanishes from the world of men, living in the swamp, learning to shapeshift, losing count of the moons and of his old name. |
| 3 TA | Kazzimir has re-emerged from the mire and joined the Chosen Party, roughly two decades after his death — old enough that almost no one from his mercenary days would recognize him. The identity of "Kazzimir the mercenary" is shed entirely. |

## The plague-girl

In Year 2 of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Third Age\|Third Age]], the Chosen Party met a plague-stricken girl from the Swamp of Sh'rek — one whom [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] had known before. She warned of shadows stirring in her homeland and led the party south. She was, in truth, the **vessel of the Necrotic King**, reborn from the mists of the old war.

## The battle

At the swamp, the King's true nature was revealed. The battle cracked the still waters: undead legions from the **Rizzhian War** rose at his call, and the fight raged beneath a storm of necrotic light and bubbling mire. In the chaos, a colossal hand of black tentacles burst from the depths and dragged the paladin who hunted [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Levren\|Levren]] screaming beneath the surface.
