The **Swamp of Sh'rek** is a plague-haunted mire of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] and the hidden cradle of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]] — a place where the dead of an old war never truly rested.

## The plague-girl

In Year 2 of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Third Age\|Third Age]], the Chosen Party met a plague-stricken girl from the Swamp of Sh'rek — one whom [[rosvania/Wiki/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] had known before. She warned of shadows stirring in her homeland and led the party south. She was, in truth, the **vessel of the Necrotic King**, reborn from the mists of the old war.

## The battle

At the swamp, the King's true nature was revealed. The battle cracked the still waters: undead legions from the **Rizzhian War** rose at his call, and the fight raged beneath a storm of necrotic light and bubbling mire. In the chaos, a colossal hand of black tentacles burst from the depths and dragged the paladin who hunted [[rosvania/Wiki/Levren\|Levren]] screaming beneath the surface.
