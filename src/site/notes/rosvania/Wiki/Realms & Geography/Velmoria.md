**Velmoria** is the capital of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]], the golden city at the heart of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] where the Sun Emperor rules from sun-towers of Solarite-bright stone.

## History

| Years | Event |
| --- | --- |
| Before 313 SA | A nameless deep-water bay on the western coast, used on and off by traders and fisherfolk through the long chaos that followed [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Menor]]'s collapse at the end of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/First Age\|First Age]] (982 FA). No permanent settlement — just a good place to shelter a ship. |
| 313 SA | **Duke Aurelian** — likely a claimant with roots in fallen Menor, which earns the Dominion its later epithet "the new Menor" — plants his banner at the bay and founds Velmoria as a fortified harbor town, uniting the scattered farms and fishing camps of the region. The same year, Tuzland's [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Taner\|Taner]] begins uniting the valleys to the west — a broader wave of rebuilding across the continent. |
| 313–689 SA | **The Ducal Line.** Aurelian's descendants hold Velmoria as their seat for the better part of four centuries, growing it from a walled harbor town into the largest port on the western coast. A quieter, consolidating era: farmland expansion inland, trade routes reaching toward Tuzland and the ruins of Menor. |
| 690–974 SA | **The Fractured Duchy.** Rival branches of House Aurelian carve the Duchy into competing city-states — the same dark stretch that fragments Tuzland in its own civil war, a shared continental collapse. Velmoria likely remains the largest of these city-states, but it no longer commands the others. |
| 975 SA | **The Reunification.** Emperor Aerion ends nearly three centuries of fragmentation, reconquering or absorbing the splinter city-states one by one, and crowns himself Emperor rather than Duke. Velmoria is elevated from a ducal seat to an Imperial capital; the nation is renamed the Aurelian Domain. |
| c. 1120–1300 SA | **The Golden Era of Solric.** Under Emperor Solric the city takes the shape the maps show: the Palace of the Sun, the wealthy merchant quarter now known as the City of [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Bourgeosy\|Bourgeosy]], and the Sanctum of Zerzabal all likely rise in this stretch — a capital building monuments because it can finally afford to, after three centuries of fighting itself. |
| 1301–1459 SA | **Aurelian V and the Northern Expeditions.** The Domain's strength turns outward in campaigns north — likely against border pressure from the [[rosvania/Wiki/Factions & Organizations/Silver Accord\|Silver Accord]]'s crusader-knights or raiders out of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Emberwastes\|Emberwastes]]. The harbor becomes as much a naval and logistics capital as a trade one. |
| 1460–1501 SA | **The Still Years.** [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Emperor Taryon V\|Emperor Taryon V]] rules through the quiet, stagnant final century of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Second Age\|Second Age]]. No great wars, no great growth — a capital holding steady rather than expanding. |
| 1–3 TA | **The Third Age.** The age-transition brought no succession: Taryon V still reigns three years into the new age, his throne untouched while the empire is caught between two shadows — the southern necrotic plague of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]], and the hidden counsel of [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vorun Khar\|Vorun Khar]] in his own halls. |

## The hidden counsel

Beneath the courtly smiles and priestly decrees of Velmoria, a quiet influence seeps through the halls — a whisper of counsel none can trace. The lore names it the work of the **Hidden Witness**, shaping the empire's blindness while the true peril gathers strength unseen. From the Velmorian court, [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Emperor Taryon V\|Emperor Taryon V]] calls the omens of the north mere superstition.
