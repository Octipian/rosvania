> *"Then came the deck — 'a deck of some things,' the blacksmith had called it."*

The **Deck of Some Things** is the reality-bending deck acquired from the blacksmith of [[rosvania/Wiki/Lotusden\|Lotusden]] — a card game in which every draw rewrites the world around the drawer, cruelly and without warning.

## Acquired

In Year 2 of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]], after the blacksmith's silver tongue had scammed [[rosvania/Wiki/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Gerard\|Gerard]] (while [[rosvania/Wiki/Levren\|Levren]] secured a single weapon worth the gold), the deck changed hands. The party was warned only by its name — *"a deck of some things."*

## Known draws

- **Levren's draw** summoned a vengeful paladin fated to hunt him — a curse that followed the party until the paladin was dragged beneath the waters of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Swamp of Sh'rek\|Swamp of Sh'rek]] by a colossal hand of black tentacles.
- **Kazzimir's draw** tore open a rift that cast him into another realm for a full day — he returned mid-battle against the [[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]], falling from the sky atop the monster itself.

## Nature

The deck's full power is unplumbed. Each card drawn bends reality — but the cost and the limits of those bends are measured only in the moments they create.
