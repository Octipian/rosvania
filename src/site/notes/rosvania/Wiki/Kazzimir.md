**Kazzimir** is the warrior of the Chosen Party marked by divine scars — a healer from the wetlands of [[rosvania/Wiki/Zaporizzia\|Zaporizzia]], and a man whose past keeps finding him.

## The party's beginning

Kazzimir was drawn into the Chosen Party on the road to [[rosvania/Wiki/Arglaxx\|Arglaxx]]. His healing magic became a double-edged blade in the cursed forest: at the Trial of Will, his healing only strengthened the phantom illusion of [[rosvania/Wiki/Golgoth\|Golgoth]], feeding it life — until [[rosvania/Wiki/Floris Flex\|Floris Flex]] discerned and shattered the deceit.

## The Ashrend Peaks

Suspicion and fatigue clouded Kazzimir's judgment at the [[rosvania/Wiki/Ashrend Peaks\|Ashrend Peaks]], where blades were drawn before words against four goblins. That night the vengeance came — a horde and a mountain troll whose hurled boulder split the earth, cutting him off from his comrades. Wounded, he fled down the slope; [[rosvania/Wiki/Gerard\|Gerard]]'s rope pulled him to safety.

## The Deck

In [[rosvania/Wiki/Lotusden\|Lotusden]], Kazzimir's draw from the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deck of Some Things\|Deck of Some Things]] tore open a rift that cast him into another realm for a **full day** — he returned mid-battle against the [[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]], falling from the sky atop the monster itself, and took more wounds from his allies' misfires than from the enemy's blows.

## The sea

In Year 3, Kazzimir dove into the sea and transformed into a giant octopus — only for [[rosvania/Wiki/Captain Serak\|Captain Serak]] to seize his mind and force him to attack his own allies. A cosmic vision from the cursed orb then locked his mind in place; Captain [[rosvania/Wiki/Salazar\|Salazar]] freed him by smashing the orb to pieces. He fought on through the Trial of Combat — and the party's path now leads back toward his homeland's prophecy: *finish the Trials.*
