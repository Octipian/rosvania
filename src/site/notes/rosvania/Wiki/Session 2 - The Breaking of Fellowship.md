The second session of the campaign — the breaking of the original fellowship, and the rise of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]].

## The breaking

At dawn, [[rosvania/Wiki/Floris Flex\|Floris Flex]] departed. The remaining three — [[rosvania/Wiki/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]], [[rosvania/Wiki/Gerard\|Gerard]], and [[rosvania/Wiki/Levren\|Levren]] — soon met a plague-stricken girl from the [[rosvania/Wiki/Swamp of Sh'rek\|Swamp of Sh'rek]], one Kazzimir had known before. She warned of shadows stirring in her homeland, and they set out toward the swamp.

## The Ashrend Peaks

Beneath a moonless sky, the party met four goblins on the pass. Suspicion and fatigue clouded Kazzimir's judgment; blades were drawn before words. When the skirmish ended, only one goblin escaped.

That night vengeance came: a horde of dozens and a mountain troll large enough to shake the cliffs. A hurled boulder split the earth, cutting Kazzimir off from his comrades. From the woods rose an **Eldritch Tree Guardian**, colliding with the troll in a battle that shook the valley. Gerard cast a rope and pulled Kazzimir to safety. They escaped with neither horses nor sleep.

## The City of Lotuses

By dawn they reached [[rosvania/Wiki/Lotusden\|Lotusden]], a tiefling city of crimson lanterns and thieves' honor. There met **[[rosvania/Wiki/Tarres Veylen\|Tarres Veylen]]**, who guided them to a blacksmith of strange artifacts — whose silver tongue scammed both Kazzimir and Gerard, while Levren secured a single weapon worth the gold.

Then came the **[[rosvania/Wiki/Deck of Some Things\|Deck of Some Things]]**. Levren's draw summoned a vengeful paladin fated to hunt him; Kazzimir's draw tore open a rift that cast him into another realm for a full day.

## The Swamp of Sh'rek

At the swamp, the plague-girl revealed her true self — the vessel of the **[[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]]**, reborn from the mists of the old war. Undead legions from the Rizzhian War rose at his call. Kazzimir returned mid-battle, falling from the sky atop the monster itself, taking more wounds from allies' misfires than enemy blows.

When the swamp fell silent, the paladin who sought Levren's death rose from the reeds — before a colossal hand of black tentacles dragged him screaming beneath the surface.

## The return of prophecy

The survivors returned to [[rosvania/Wiki/Zaporizzia\|Zaporizzia]], Kazzimir's homeland. From the shadows of the old streets emerged the same wizard who had once studied the [[rosvania/Wiki/Cursed Map\|Cursed Map]] — hollow-eyed, trembling:

*"Vharuk wakes again. The Dreaming God stirs beneath the veil. You must finish the Trials. Only then will the Protectors of the Realm accept you… and only then may the world stand a chance."*

The winds from the north howled once more. The new age was accelerating.
