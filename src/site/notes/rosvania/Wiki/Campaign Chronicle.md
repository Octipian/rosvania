# Campaign Chronicle

> *"The new age had not only begun — it was accelerating."*

The record of the Rosvania campaign — the adventures of the Chosen Party in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]].

## The Chosen Party

- [[rosvania/Wiki/Floris Flex\|Floris Flex]] — scholar of forbidden arts *(departed after Session 1)*
- [[rosvania/Wiki/Gerard\|Gerard]] — reckless sellsword
- [[rosvania/Wiki/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] — warrior marked by divine scars, of [[rosvania/Wiki/Zaporizzia\|Zaporizzia]]
- [[rosvania/Wiki/Levren\|Levren]] — the watcher from the shadows, allegiance unknown

## The goal

Recover the lost amulet — a relic of the old wars said to grant one's truest desire, or unmake its bearer — and complete the Five Trials so that the [[rosvania/Wiki/Protectors of the Realm\|Protectors of the Realm]] accept the Chosen Party. For, as the hollow-eyed wizard said: *"Only then may the world stand a chance."*
