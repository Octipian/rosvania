> # Erynos
> **Titles** — The Tidebreaker, Lord of the Seas
> **Type** — Deity ([[rosvania/Wiki/Firstborn Wardens\|Firstborn Warden]])

> *"Whose wrath and mercy flowed as one."*

**Erynos** is the **Tidebreaker**, Lord of the Seas — Warden of the waters, whose wrath and mercy flow as one. Of all the loyal Wardens, he is the most terrible when provoked, and the most recently seen.

## Nature

Erynos is the ocean itself: patient, vast, nourishing in calm and annihilating in storm. He does not forgive the arrogance of mortals who reach for dominion over his realm.

## History

- **The [[rosvania/Wiki/Shattered Sea War\|Shattered Sea War]] (803–982 FA):** When Old Tuzland and [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Menor]] waged war against the Aquatic Empires out of greed, Erynos rose in wrath and cast a divine tsunami — erasing half of Tuzland's coast, creating the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzland and the Whispering Sands\|Whispering Sands]], and drowning [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Menor]]'s capital by the returning wave. The collapse of Menor opened the east to [[rosvania/Wiki/Khar'doth the Uniter\|Khar'doth the Uniter]].
- **Year 3 TA (the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]]):** Erynos manifested above the sea during the party's clash with [[rosvania/Wiki/Captain Serak\|Captain Serak]], the Leviathan Caller. Serak withdrew at once, unwilling to challenge a god. The Tuzlan fleet behind him — fifty ships screaming of a holy *Reconquista* — fired into the storm; Erynos annihilated them all in moments, leaving a graveyard of burning timbers and silence.

## Legacy

- The sea lanes of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] carry his memory: legends of the drowned capitals, the Whispering Sands, and the fleet that challenged a god and vanished in fire.
- The [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Corsairs\|Tuzlean Corsairs]], masters of the coasts, have learned an uneasy respect for his domain — while the [[rosvania/Wiki/Frost-Reavers\|Frost-Reavers]] raid his seas with black ships, unanswered.
