> *"The survivors, battered and mud-soaked, returned to Kazzimir's homeland (Zaporizzia)."*

**Zaporizzia** is the marshy homeland of [[rosvania/Wiki/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]], the warrior of the Chosen Party marked by divine scars. Little is recorded of its founding; the lore remembers it as the place of the party's first turnings.

## Known events

- After the battle at the [[rosvania/Wiki/Swamp of Sh'rek\|Swamp of Sh'rek]], the party returned to the old streets of Zaporizzia.
- There, from the shadows, emerged the same wizard who had once studied the [[rosvania/Wiki/Cursed Map\|Cursed Map]] — his eyes hollow, his voice trembling. He spoke the words that set the age's course: *"Vharuk wakes again. The Dreaming God stirs beneath the veil. You must finish the Trials. Only then will the Protectors of the Realm accept you."*
