> *"A shambling, drunken captain named Salazar, who swayed like a mast in a storm and smelled like he'd drowned in stale rum."*

**Salazar** is the drunken captain of the **[[rosvania/Wiki/Good ol' Jacky\|Good ol' Jacky]]** — the man who sold the Chosen Party their cursed brigantine, smashed a reality-warping orb, and sailed through the wrath of gods with a bottle in hand.

## The sale

In Year 3 of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]], the party sought passage from the coastal port near the Trial of Will. They found Salazar, who offered them the *Good ol' Jacky* — a brigantine so battered it looked like it might sink from being stared at too hard. It was miserable. It was cheap. They bought it. They drank. Hard.

## The battle with Serak

They awoke to choking fog and exploding cannons. Through the battle with [[rosvania/Wiki/Captain Serak\|Captain Serak]] — in which the Jacky refused to sink — Salazar provided the age's finest moment of practical wisdom: when [[rosvania/Wiki/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] was seized and locked in place by a cosmic vision from the cursed orb, Salazar solved the problem the only way he knew how — by smashing the orb to pieces.

## Current state

When the party returned to the docks, bruised yet triumphant, they found the Good ol' Jacky waiting — and Salazar sprawled drunkenly across the deck like a man who had slept through the end of the world, bottle in hand, ready for whatever nightmare came next.
