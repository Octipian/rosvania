> *"A city of dust and deceit."*

**Arglaxx** is a city of dust and deceit on the roads of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] — the place where fate first gathered the heroes of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]].

## The fateful encounter

Upon the wind-swept road to Arglaxx, three wanderers — [[rosvania/Wiki/Floris Flex\|Floris Flex]], the scholar of forbidden arts; [[rosvania/Wiki/Gerard\|Gerard]], the reckless sellsword; and [[rosvania/Wiki/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]], a warrior marked by divine scars — were drawn together amid a goblin ambush, with [[rosvania/Wiki/Levren\|Levren]] watching from the shadows. The battle was ended not by man nor spell but by the arrival of [[rosvania/Wiki/Golgoth\|Golgoth]], the last of the elder drakes.

## The city itself

In Arglaxx, the party found an escaped goblin clutching a map. The parchment pulsed with a darkness older than any mortal curse — the **[[rosvania/Wiki/Cursed Map\|Cursed Map]]**, whose ink moved like living shadow. From Arglaxx, the companions rode south to [[rosvania/Wiki/Bourgeosy\|Bourgeosy]].
