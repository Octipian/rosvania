> # War of Life
> rf-imgslot
> ![Portrait Placeholder.png\|180](/img/user/rosvania/Wiki/img/Portrait%20Placeholder.png)
> **Type** — Divine civil war
> **Belligerents** — [[rosvania/Wiki/Alliance of Life\|Alliance of Life]] vs. [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]], the [[rosvania/Wiki/The Unmade\|The Unmade]], Orc and Goblin legions
> **Period** — Ended in the year 10 FA
> **Key place** — The [[rosvania/Wiki/Black Spire of Mourning\|Black Spire of Mourning]]

> *"The heavens burned. Mountains shattered. The Firstborn themselves walked among mortals, and their struggle would mark the first chapter of creation's long sorrow."*

The **War of Life** — the Great Betrayal made war — was the first great war of creation, waged when gods walked among mortals. It was sparked by the fall of [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]] and decided the fate of the world for all ages to come.

## Causes

[[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]], Warden of Silence, had grown to believe life itself a flaw. In secret he forged the [[rosvania/Wiki/Orcs\|Orcs]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Goblins\|Goblins]], corrupted [[rosvania/Wiki/Namarielle\|Namarielle]]'s essence into the [[rosvania/Wiki/The Unmade\|The Unmade]], and proclaimed the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doctrine of Desolation\|Doctrine of Desolation]]. When his treachery was discovered, he declared war upon all life — *"to cleanse is to save."* The [[rosvania/Wiki/Unseen Father\|Unseen Father]], heartbroken, withdrew and decreed that His children must defend what He had given them.

## The Alliance of Life

The loyal Wardens and the mortal races united under the radiant banners of [[rosvania/Wiki/Solareth\|Solareth]]:

- The **[[rosvania/Wiki/Elves\|Elves]]** of Vael'Tharion, guided by the direct voice of [[rosvania/Wiki/Vael'Thara\|Vael'Thara]], became the Alliance's spellweavers and healers.
- The **[[rosvania/Wiki/Dwarves\|Dwarves]]** of [[rosvania/Wiki/Durakheim\|Durakheim]], under [[rosvania/Wiki/Korran-Dur\|Korran-Dur]], forged [[rosvania/Wiki/Metals of Rosvania\|light-forged steel]] — the only metal that could wound [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]'s kin.
- The **[[rosvania/Wiki/Men\|Men]]** of Tuzland sent but a small host — yet their greatest champions, the Demigods of the Hammered Sun led by Tuzlak the Great King and the mighty Big G, fought at the fore.

## The final blow

In the year 10 FA, the combined hosts stormed the **[[rosvania/Wiki/Black Spire of Mourning\|Black Spire of Mourning]]** in the far north. Solareth struck the final blow, shattering Vharuk's body — but his essence could not be slain. [[rosvania/Wiki/Athera\|Athera]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Namarielle\|Namarielle]] bound his soul beneath the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]], sealing his prison in eternal night.

## Aftermath

- The Wardens withdrew, their power dimmed. Gods never again walked openly among mortals.
- The Orcs and Goblins scattered — to [[rosvania/Wiki/Gorthul-Maar\|Gorthul-Maar]] and the [[rosvania/Wiki/Emberwastes\|Emberwastes]].
- The world entered the [[rosvania/Wiki/First Age\|First Age]], never again whole.
