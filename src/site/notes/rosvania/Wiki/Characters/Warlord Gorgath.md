**Warlord Gorgath** is the present master of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Orcs\|Orcs]] of [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Gorthul-Maar\|Gorthul-Maar]] — the aggressive war-leader whose raids define the borders of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]] in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Third Age\|Third Age]].

## The rise

In the Cracking Age of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Second Age\|Second Age]], with the disappearance of the Aurelian legions in the north read as weakness, Gorgath united the clans and launched audacious raids across the border.

## The Third Age

By Year 1 TA, the Orc raids under Gorgath were a serious threat on the Aurelian border — a real, burning conflict that consumed the armies and attention of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Emperor Taryon V\|Emperor Taryon V]]. This very real war is the perfect distraction: it keeps the world's eyes southward while the true darkness gathers strength in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Doomlands\|Doomlands]] to the north.

Unwittingly or not, Gorgath is the shield of [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vorun Khar\|Vorun Khar]]'s work — the noise that hides the silence.
