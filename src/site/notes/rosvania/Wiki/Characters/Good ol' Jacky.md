The **Good ol' Jacky** is the cursed brigantine of Captain [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Salazar\|Salazar]] — the ship so battered it looked like it might sink from being stared at too hard, and yet, by some stubborn magic, the ship that cannot die.

## The purchase

At the coastal port near the Trial of Will, the Chosen Party sought passage and found Salazar. He offered the *Good ol' Jacky* — miserable, cheap, and somehow upright. They bought it. They drank. Hard.

## The battle

They awoke to choking fog and exploding cannons. Ambushed by a Tuzlan Corsair, then caught between [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Captain Serak\|Captain Serak]]'s spectral *Drownkeeper* and his mind-seized octopus ally, the Jacky should have splintered apart. It refused. The cursed brigantine endured, suffered, and lived — until the sky split open and [[rosvania/Wiki/Erynos\|Erynos]] arrived.

## Current state

When the party returned from the Trial of Combat, bruised yet triumphant, they found the Good ol' Jacky waiting — with Salazar sprawled across the deck, bottle in hand, ready for whatever nightmare came next.
