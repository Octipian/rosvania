**Golgoth** is the last of the elder drakes of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] — a being of ancient purpose who ended the goblin ambush on the road to [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Arglaxx\|Arglaxx]] and set the heroes of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Third Age\|Third Age]] upon their path.

## The arrival

As steel clashed and magic flared on the road to Arglaxx, the battle was ended not by man nor spell — but by Golgoth's arrival. His wings blotted the sun; his eyes burned with ancient purpose.

## The quest

In the ruinous silence that followed, Golgoth spoke. He was the final guardian of a dying lineage, burdened with one last task: to recover a lost amulet — a relic of the old wars said to grant one's truest desire, or unmake them through it. Thus were [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Floris Flex\|Floris Flex]], [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Gerard\|Gerard]], [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Levren\|Levren]] bound together as the first Chosen Party of the new age, each drawn by fate, curiosity, or the promise of redemption.

## Later appearances

Golgoth reappeared after the Trial of Will in the cursed forest, wings black against the dusk, to confirm the trial was passed and the path ahead open. The illusion of him conjured by the [[rosvania/Wiki/Artifacts & Items/Cursed Map\|Cursed Map]]'s malice proved the drake's name travels before him — and his purpose with it.
