The **Silver Marches** are the northeastern plains of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] — the ancient border ground between the realms of Men and the black plains of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Orcs\|Orcs]].

## History

- In the [[rosvania/Wiki/First Age\|First Age]], the Orcs of [[rosvania/Wiki/Khar'doth the Uniter\|Khar'doth the Uniter]] ruled these plains from the banners of [[rosvania/Wiki/Gorthul-Maar\|Gorthul-Maar]].
- In the Forgeborn Era of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Second Age\|Second Age]], the [[rosvania/Wiki/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]] pushed the hordes north **beyond** the Marches, into the frozen highlands, and sealed its border with a massive wall of fortifications.
- In the Cracking Age, Warlord [[rosvania/Wiki/Warlord Gorgath\|Gorgath]]'s raiders tested the wall again and again.

## The Third Age

The Marches are a hard land of garrisons, watch-fires, and raids. Its defenders hold the line against Gorgath's hordes while — unknown to them — the true darkness gathers behind them in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]].
