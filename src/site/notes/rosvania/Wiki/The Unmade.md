> # The Unmade
> **Type** — Beings neither living nor dead
> **Creator** — [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]], from the stolen essence of [[rosvania/Wiki/Namarielle\|Namarielle]]
> **Nature** — Perfect in their emptiness
> **Threat** — "Legions that do not sleep"
> **Where** — Beneath the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]]

> *"Beings neither living nor dead, perfect in their emptiness."*

The **Unmade** are the first and greatest corruption of [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]'s fall — beings neither living nor dead, forged when he stole the life-essence of [[rosvania/Wiki/Namarielle\|Namarielle]], the Lifebringer, and twisted it into hollow perfection.

## Origins

Before his betrayal was revealed, Vharuk took from Namarielle the breath of renewal and corrupted it. Where she made growth, he made emptiness: beings whose stillness was flawless because nothing lived within them. When the Wardens learned of this treachery, the heavens trembled — and the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]] began.

## Nature

- **Neither living nor dead** — they exist in the hollow stillness Vharuk adored; death cannot claim what never truly lived.
- **Perfect in emptiness** — empty of passion, of will, of the noise of life; they are the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doctrine of Desolation\|Doctrine of Desolation]] made flesh.
- **Their master's kin** — only [[rosvania/Wiki/Metals of Rosvania\|light-forged steel]] could wound them in the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]].

## History

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]]:** The [[rosvania/Wiki/Alliance of Life\|Alliance of Life]] fought the Unmade across the burning heavens; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] of [[rosvania/Wiki/Durakheim\|Durakheim]] forged the only weapon that could harm them.
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]]:** Whispers among the scattered survivors of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Protectors of the Realm\|Protectors of the Realm]] tell of a stirring host — "legions that do not sleep," waiting beneath the aurora-less sky of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]], as the First Seal breaks.
