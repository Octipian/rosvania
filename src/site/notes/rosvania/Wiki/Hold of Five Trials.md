> *"A realm of warriors bound by oath rather than crown."*

The **Hold of Five Trials** is a highland city and order of warriors in [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]], founded in rebellion against the petty crowns of the shattered [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzland and the Whispering Sands\|Tuzland]]. Its champions are bound by oath alone.

## Founding

From the wreck of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Civil War\|Tuzlean Civil War]], the surviving captains and soldiers who refused to bow to any crown founded the Hold and declared independence. Its masters remain neutral ground — "bound by oath to no crown" — and are counted among the most feared warriors of the land.

## The Trials

The Hold gives its name to the five trials by which warriors prove themselves — the road of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Protectors of the Realm\|Protectors of the Realm]]. In Year 3 of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]], the Chosen Party entered the **Trial of Combat**: a brutal 3v3 tournament judged by [[rosvania/Wiki/Josephine Kane\|Josephine Kane]], [[rosvania/Wiki/Vorlag\|Vorlag]], Commander of the Protectors, and [[rosvania/Wiki/Brother Tarsis\|Brother Tarsis]], a dwarven warrior-priest carrying a hammer forged from the [[rosvania/Wiki/Metals of Rosvania\|Deep's Gift]]. Through grit and blood, the party earned victory — unlocking the path to the next trials.

## The Third Age

The Hold's masters sense the turning of the world and watch the encroaching dark with wary resolve, keeping their gate open to worthy champions while the storm gathers in the north.
