The **High Synod** is the governing council of the Elven realm of [[rosvania/Wiki/Etharion\|Etharion]] — the body that has kept the deep forests closed to the world since the shame of the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of the Radiant Blades\|War of the Radiant Blades]].

## The endless debate

For ages, the Synod has debated one question in endless twilight: whether the Elves should stay hidden, guarding the roots of their realm, or walk once more beside mortals as in the days of the [[rosvania/Wiki/First Age\|First Age]]. The debate was a luxury of peace — delayed and delayed again.

## The Third Age

The luxury ended when the Eldertree began to shriek. The rise of the **[[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]]** — a profound mockery of life and perversion of [[rosvania/Wiki/Namarielle\|Namarielle]]'s domain — shattered the Synod's endless debate. The time for isolation is over; a decision, long delayed, must now be made. Its choice may shape the fate of all the living.
