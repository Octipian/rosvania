**Tarres Veylen** is a rogue of [[rosvania/Wiki/Lotusden\|Lotusden]] who guided the Chosen Party through the tiefling city of crimson lanterns in Year 2 of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]].

## Role

Tarres led the party to a blacksmith known to deal in strange artifacts. The blacksmith's silver tongue proved sharper than his blades — he scammed both [[rosvania/Wiki/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Gerard\|Gerard]], while [[rosvania/Wiki/Levren\|Levren]], more cautious, secured a single weapon worth the gold. The encounter ended with the party acquiring the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deck of Some Things\|Deck of Some Things]].

Tarres's own allegiance, and whether the ambush that followed was fortune or design, remains unrecorded.
