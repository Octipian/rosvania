> # Tieflings
> **Type** — Lesser kindred
> **Origin** — Unrecorded in the lore
> **Homelands** — Cities such as [[rosvania/Wiki/Lotusden\|Lotusden]]
> **Nature** — Crimson lanterns and thieves' honor
> **Known places** — Lotusden, the City of Lotuses

> *"A tiefling city of crimson lanterns and thieves' honor."*

The **Tieflings** are a sharp-edged people of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]], keepers of the shadow cities — places of crimson lanterns, cunning, and a code of honor that runs beneath the law. Their origin is unrecorded in the lore.

## Known places

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Lotusden\|Lotusden]]** — the City of Lotuses, where the Chosen Party met [[rosvania/Wiki/Tarres Veylen\|Tarres Veylen]] and acquired the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deck of Some Things\|Deck of Some Things]] in Year 2 of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]].
