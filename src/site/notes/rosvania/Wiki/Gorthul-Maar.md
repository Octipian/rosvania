**Gorthul-Maar** is the black northeastern plain that is the ancestral home of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Orcs\|Orcs]] — the land to which they retreated after the fall of [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]], and from which they have twice risen to challenge the world.

## History

- The Orcs were forged by [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]] from the great wolves and serpents of the mountains; after his defeat in the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]], they scattered to Gorthul-Maar, the harshest corner of the world.
- In the [[rosvania/Wiki/First Age\|First Age]], the tribes were united by **[[rosvania/Wiki/Khar'doth the Uniter\|Khar'doth the Uniter]]**, who conquered the fallen east after the [[rosvania/Wiki/Shattered Sea War\|Shattered Sea War]] and ruled the plains for a golden age of Orc-kind.
- In the [[rosvania/Wiki/Second Age\|Second Age]], the [[rosvania/Wiki/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]] pushed the hordes back north of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Silver Marches\|Silver Marches]].
- In the Cracking Age, Warlord **[[rosvania/Wiki/Warlord Gorgath\|Gorgath]]** rose and began audacious raids against the Aurelian border — a threat that still burns in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]].

## The Third Age

Gorgath's raids are a serious, constant threat on the borders of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]] — a very real conflict that serves as the perfect distraction, keeping the eyes of emperors fixed south while the true darkness gathers in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]] to the north.
