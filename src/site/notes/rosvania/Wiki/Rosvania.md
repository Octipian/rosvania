**Rosvania** is the mortal realm forged by the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Unseen Father\|Unseen Father]] through His Three Decrees, and the stage upon which the history of gods and mortals unfolds. It was created as a perfect kingdom of order and life; its history since has been the slow unraveling of that perfection.

## Creation

By the **First Decree**, earth was divided from sky — the mountains of [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Durakheim\|Durakheim]] rose like crowns of stone, and the seas were carved about the land. By the **Second Decree**, the sun and stars were set in their dance, granting light, warmth, and time. By the **Third Decree**, life took root: forests, beasts, and finally the elder races — the Elves from starlight, the Dwarves from mountain-heart, and Men from the soil between them.

To tend His creation, the Father forged from His own essence the eight [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Firstborn Wardens\|Firstborn Wardens]].

## Geography

- The **frozen north**: the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Doomlands\|Doomlands]], prison of [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vharuk\|Vharuk]], whence creeps the Breath of Vharuk — an unending blizzard that devours light and life.
- The **north-central mountains**: [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Durakheim\|Durakheim]], the sealed halls of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Dwarves\|Dwarves]].
- The **northwest**: [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Etharion\|Etharion]], the deep forests of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Elves\|Elves]], home of the Eldertree.
- The **center**: the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]], golden empire of Men, ruled from [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Velmoria\|Velmoria]] by [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Emperor Taryon V\|Emperor Taryon V]].
- The **southwest**: [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Tuzland and the Whispering Sands\|Tuzland and the Whispering Sands]], the shattered remnants of old empires, ruled by the [[rosvania/Wiki/Factions & Organizations/Tuzlean Corsairs\|Tuzlean Corsairs]] and home to the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Hold of Five Trials\|Hold of Five Trials]].
- The **east**: the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Silver Marches\|Silver Marches]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Vael'nore\|Vael'nore]], lands of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Factions & Organizations/Silver Accord\|Silver Accord]], bright with trade — and the black plains of [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Gorthul-Maar\|Gorthul-Maar]], homeland of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Orcs\|Orcs]].
- The **south**: the burning woods of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Emberwastes\|Emberwastes]], where [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Goblins\|Goblin]] [[rosvania/Wiki/Factions & Organizations/Goblin Syndicates\|Syndicates]] hold sway.
- Elsewhere: [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Zaporizzia\|Zaporizzia]], the swampy homeland of [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]]; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Swamp of Sh'rek\|Swamp of Sh'rek]], cradle of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]]; and the drowned realm of [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Menor]] beneath the waves.

## History in brief

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Age Before Ages\|Before ages]]** — the world is forged; [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vharuk\|Vharuk]] falls to pride, forges the [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Orcs\|Orcs]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Goblins\|Goblins]], and betrays creation in the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]].
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/First Age\|First Age]] (0–982 FA)** — the Age of Gods and Empires: the [[rosvania/Wiki/Factions & Organizations/Alliance of Life\|Alliance of Life]] triumphs, empires rise in pride, and [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Erynos\|Erynos]] drowns the greedy powers in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Shattered Sea War\|Shattered Sea War]].
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Second Age\|Second Age]] (0–1501 SA)** — the Age of Mortals: ash, rebuilding, the [[War of Bloodlines\|Tuzlean Civil War]], the quiet reign of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]], and the long, false peace of the Still Years.
- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Third Age\|Third Age]] (1 TA– )** — the Age of Shadow and Return: the Seal of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Doomlands\|Doomlands]] cracks, and a new war of life and silence begins.

## The present state (Year 3 TA)

With the breaking of the First Seal, the Still Years ended. The [[rosvania/Wiki/Factions & Organizations/Frost-Reavers\|Frost-Reavers]] sail black ships upon storm-torn seas; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/The Unmade\|The Unmade]] wait beneath an aurora-less sky; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]] stirs in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Swamp of Sh'rek\|Swamp of Sh'rek]]; and a hidden hand — the Hidden Witness, [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vorun Khar\|Vorun Khar]] — bends the courts of Men toward blindness. Against this rising dark, the [[rosvania/Wiki/Factions & Organizations/Protectors of the Realm\|Protectors of the Realm]] test the new heroes of the age: the Chosen Party of [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Floris Flex\|Floris Flex]], [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Gerard\|Gerard]], [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Levren\|Levren]].
