> # Serathis
> **Titles** — The Shadowbinder, Keeper of Secrets
> **Type** — Deity ([[rosvania/Wiki/Firstborn Wardens\|Firstborn Warden]])
> **Domain** — Shadow, secrecy, veils
> **Affiliation** — [[rosvania/Wiki/Firstborn Wardens\|Firstborn Wardens]]
> **Nature** — Not darkness, but the veil that protects

> *"Veiling what must remain unseen."*

**Serathis** is the **Shadowbinder**, Keeper of Secrets — the Warden who veils what must remain unseen. Unlike [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]'s hollow stillness, Serathis's shadow is protective: the dark behind which truth may rest unmolested.

## Nature

Serathis is the hush between words, the locked door, the secret kept. The Shadowbinder holds that not all things are meant to be known at once — and that some truths must ripen in darkness before they may face the light.

## History

- Serathis stood loyal through the [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]], serving as the [[rosvania/Wiki/Alliance of Life\|Alliance of Life]]'s unseen hand — keeper of its plans and veiler of its movements.
- After the war, Serathis withdrew with the other Wardens. The Shadowbinder's continued silence is a cause of both comfort and dread: some suspect Serathis still veils the world's true state from its people.

## Relations

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]]:** Serathis is his opposite — shadow that shields versus silence that empties. Some theologians hold that the Shadowbinder was the only Warden who understood the Stillborn Flame's loneliness, and the one most grieved by his fall.
- The [[rosvania/Wiki/Cursed Map\|Cursed Map]]'s living ink — "a black magic rarely seen since the First Age" — is whispered by some to bear the Shadowbinder's mark, veiling its true purpose.
