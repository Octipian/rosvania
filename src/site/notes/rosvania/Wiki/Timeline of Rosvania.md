# Timeline of Rosvania

> *"The new age had not only begun — it was accelerating."*

This is a chronological index of the known history of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]], from the forging of the world to the present year of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]].

## First Age (0–982 FA) — Age of Gods and Empires

| Years | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 0–10 FA | The [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]] ends at the [[rosvania/Wiki/Black Spire of Mourning\|Black Spire of Mourning]]; [[rosvania/Wiki/Vharuk\|Vharuk]] is sealed in the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]]. The Wardens withdraw. |
| 11–480 FA | The Rebuilding. The Orcs retreat to [[rosvania/Wiki/Gorthul-Maar\|Gorthul-Maar]], the Goblins to the [[rosvania/Wiki/Emberwastes\|Emberwastes]]. [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Menor]] arises east of Tuzland. |
| 481–802 FA | The Age of Empire and Shadow. The [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Solar Empire of Menor]] wars on the Orcs; the Goblins found the [[rosvania/Wiki/Goblin Syndicates\|Goblin Syndicates]] in [[rosvania/Wiki/Vael'nore\|Vael'nore]]. |
| 539–561 FA | [[rosvania/Wiki/War of the Radiant Blades\|War of the Radiant Blades]] — the Elves of the Radiant Dominion march against Men; the fall of Queen [[rosvania/Wiki/Vael'Nir\|Vael'Nir]]. |
| 803–982 FA | [[rosvania/Wiki/Shattered Sea War\|Shattered Sea War]] — [[rosvania/Wiki/Erynos\|Erynos]] drowns the coasts of Tuzland and Menor; [[rosvania/Wiki/Khar'doth the Uniter\|Khar'doth the Uniter]] conquers the fallen east. |

## Second Age (0–1501 SA) — Age of Mortals

| Years | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 0–312 SA | The Years of Ash — dark age of famine and wandering; Orc dominion in the east; the slave trade of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Goblin Syndicates\|Goblin Syndicates]] flourishes. |
| 313–689 SA | The Forgeborn Era — the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Concord\|Tuzlean Concord]] rises; scholars rediscover [[rosvania/Wiki/Metals of Rosvania\|Solarite]]; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]] is founded and drives the Orcs north. |
| 690–974 SA | The War of Bloodlines — the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Civil War\|Tuzlean Civil War]]; the age of [[rosvania/Wiki/Taner IV\|Taner IV]] ends in division; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Corsairs\|Tuzlean Corsairs]] are born; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Hold of Five Trials\|Hold of Five Trials]] declares independence. |
| 975–1300 SA | The Quiet Reign — Aurelian's wall; the Syndicates' gladiator trade peaks under Batrok; Tuzland fractures into pirate enclaves. |
| 1301–1459 SA | The Cracking Age — the seals weaken; Aurelian legions vanish in the north; Warlord [[rosvania/Wiki/Warlord Gorgath\|Gorgath]] raids the border; the [[rosvania/Wiki/Protectors of the Realm\|Protectors of the Realm]] are forged. |
| 1460–1501 SA | The Still Years — forty winters of tension; [[rosvania/Wiki/Vorun Khar\|Vorun Khar]] walks among kings as the Hidden Witness. |
