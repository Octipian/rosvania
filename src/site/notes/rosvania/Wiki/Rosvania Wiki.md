# Rosvania Wiki

> *"Thus was formed Rosvania, the Father's perfect kingdom, a harmony of life and silence."*
> — The King's Decree, from the [lore of Rosvania](/img/user/rosvania/Rosvania%20lore.pdf)

Welcome to the **Rosvania Wiki**, an encyclopedia of the world of Rosvania — its gods, its ages, its wars and its peoples; a world forged by the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Unseen Father\|Unseen Father]] and shaped by the fall of [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vharuk\|Vharuk]].

The world stands in the **[[rosvania/Wiki/Eras & Timeline/Third Age\|Third Age]]**, the Age of Shadow and Return. The seals of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Doomlands\|Doomlands]] falter, the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Doomlands\|northern blizzard]] creeps southward, and a new generation of heroes — the Chosen Party of [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Floris Flex\|Floris Flex]], [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Gerard\|Gerard]], [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] and [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Levren\|Levren]] — gathers to face the coming [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Doctrine of Desolation\|Desolation]].

> When [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vharuk\|Vharuk]], the Warden of Silence, declared that *"to cleanse is to save,"* the heavens burned. Read how the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Firstborn Wardens\|Firstborn Wardens]] and the mortal races fought the first great war of creation in [[rosvania/Wiki/War of Life\|War of Life]].

## Did you know?

- The [[rosvania/Wiki/Races/Orcs\|Orcs]] were not born of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Unseen Father\|Father's]] design — they were [[rosvania/Wiki/Deities & Gods/Vharuk\|forged]] from wolves and serpents in secret.
- [[Solar Empire of Menor\|Menor]]'s capital was drowned not once, but twice, by the wrath of [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Erynos\|Erynos]].
- The [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Good ol' Jacky\|Good ol' Jacky]], a cursed brigantine, has been *unable to sink* — even under attack from a god-touched leviathan caller.
- [[rosvania/Wiki/Characters/Levren\|Levren]] once drew a card from the [[rosvania/Wiki/Artifacts & Items/Deck of Some Things\|Deck of Some Things]] that summoned a vengeful paladin sworn to hunt him.
