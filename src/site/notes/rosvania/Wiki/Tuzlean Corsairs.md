The **Tuzlean Corsairs** are the marauders of the southern coasts — born from the wreck of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzlean Civil War\|Tuzlean Civil War]], when the surviving captains and soldiers of [[rosvania/Wiki/Tuzland and the Whispering Sands\|Tuzland]] turned to the sea.

## The black sails

From the rebellion that followed [[rosvania/Wiki/Taner IV\|Taner IV]]'s death, the Corsairs' black sails haunted the coasts for generations. They are no mere pirates: they rule the southern coasts with iron discipline, a navy of ex-soldiers with nothing left to lose.

## The Third Age

In the present age, the Corsairs clash upon the seas with the **[[rosvania/Wiki/Frost-Reavers\|Frost-Reavers]]** — the pale marauders of the north who raid south on black ships. The sea lanes of the southwest are a running war between the two fleets, unremarked by the courts that fear both.

- In Year 3 TA, a Tuzlan fleet of fifty ships — screaming of a holy *Reconquista* — fired into the storm-tossed battle of the party and the Leviathan Caller; [[rosvania/Wiki/Erynos\|Erynos]] annihilated them all in moments.
