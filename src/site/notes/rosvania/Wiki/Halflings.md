> # Halflings
> **Type** — Lesser kindred
> **Origin** — Unrecorded in the lore
> **Homelands** — Towns such as [[rosvania/Wiki/Bumblebrook\|Bumblebrook]]
> **Nature** — Warm hearths, foolish hearts
> **Known places** — Bumblebrook, a haven along the party's road

> *"A haven of warm hearths and foolish hearts."*

The **Halflings** are a small, warm-hearted people of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]], known for hearth, harvest, and hospitality. Their origin is unrecorded in the lore; they are counted among the lesser kindreds of the world.

## Known places

- **[[rosvania/Wiki/Bumblebrook\|Bumblebrook]]** — a halfling town on the road south, where the Chosen Party rested in Year 1 of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]]. There, laughter masked unease as the winds of the north howled beyond the hearth-light.
