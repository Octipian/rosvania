> *"A new brotherhood was forged in the northern highlands — The Protectors of the Realm."*

The **Protectors of the Realm** are the knightly order of the northern highlands, sworn to guard [[rosvania/Wiki/Rosvania\|Rosvania]] against evils seen and unseen. They are the last standing wall of the mortal world — and the order the Chosen Party must join to save it.

## Founding

Forged in the uneasy Cracking Age of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Second Age\|Second Age]], when the seals of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]] weakened and entire Aurelian legions vanished in the north, the Protectors were born of the belief that the world needed watchers who served no crown.

## The Five Trials

The Protectors test their would-be members through the **Five Trials**, held at the [[rosvania/Wiki/Hold of Five Trials\|Hold of Five Trials]]. In Year 3 of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Third Age\|Third Age]], the Chosen Party won the **Trial of Combat** — a brutal 3v3 tournament judged by Commander [[rosvania/Wiki/Vorlag\|Vorlag]], [[rosvania/Wiki/Brother Tarsis\|Brother Tarsis]], and [[rosvania/Wiki/Josephine Kane\|Josephine Kane]] — unlocking the path to the remaining trials.

## The Third Age

Among the scattered survivors of the order, whispers persist of a stirring host — the [[rosvania/Wiki/The Unmade\|The Unmade]], legions that do not sleep beneath the aurora-less sky of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Doomlands\|Doomlands]]. The hollow-eyed wizard of [[rosvania/Wiki/Zaporizzia\|Zaporizzia]] spoke their doctrine plainly: *"You must finish the Trials. Only then will the Protectors of the Realm accept you… and only then may the world stand a chance."*
