> *"Gerard, the reckless sellsword."*

**Gerard** is the reckless sellsword of the Chosen Party — a man of impulse who, for all his chaos, has twice held his companions together.

## In the party's beginning

Gerard was drawn into the Chosen Party on the road to [[rosvania/Wiki/Arglaxx\|Arglaxx]], where the goblin ambush was ended by [[rosvania/Wiki/Golgoth\|Golgoth]]'s arrival. His antics became legend early: in [[rosvania/Wiki/Bumblebrook\|Bumblebrook]] — testing doors, pestering locals, and nearly starting a fire — he exasperated [[rosvania/Wiki/Floris Flex\|Floris Flex]] and drove the party to a tavern where laughter masked unease.

## The Trial of Will

At the shrine, it was Gerard who defied the curse directly: he hurled the [[rosvania/Wiki/Cursed Map\|Cursed Map]] into the rift — only for Floris to catch it in an act born of pride and protection alike. The trial was satisfied, and the trust between the two men was not.

## The mountain and the swamp

At the [[rosvania/Wiki/Ashrend Peaks\|Ashrend Peaks]], when the mountain troll's boulder split the earth and cut [[rosvania/Wiki/Kazzimir\|Kazzimir]] off from his comrades, Gerard cast a rope down the slope and pulled him to safety. In the [[rosvania/Wiki/Swamp of Sh'rek\|Swamp of Sh'rek]], he fought beside the others against the [[rosvania/Wiki/Necrotic King\|Necrotic King]] — proving the reckless sellsword had become something more.

## The Trial of Combat

In Year 3, Gerard fought in the brutal 3v3 tournament at the [[rosvania/Wiki/Hold of Five Trials\|Hold of Five Trials]] and, through grit and blood, helped earn the party's victory — and the path to the remaining trials.
