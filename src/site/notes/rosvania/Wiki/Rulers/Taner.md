> *"One voice, one valley, one accord."*

**Taner** (remembered as **Taner the Builder**) is the founder of the **Tuzlean Concord** and the first of his name to call himself a Concord Great King rather than a tyrant. He rose in the wake of the fall of [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/The Big G\|the Big G]] and built the accord that bound the valleys of Tuzland together.

## The Concord

Taner's great work was not conquest but agreement: a parliament of valley lords, a standing peace, and a crown that answered to the realm rather than the reverse. The system he built would survive, with strain, for generations.

## Namesakes

His descendants would carry on the name through the golden age of the Concord — through [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Taner II\|Taner II]], [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Taner III\|Taner III]], and finally [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Taner IV\|Taner IV]], the last great king to wear the full accord.
