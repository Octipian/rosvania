**The Big G** is a title — and a warning — borne by the [[Great King of Tuzland\|Great King of Tuzland]] of the **Tuzlean Concord** who ruled during the Second Age. He was the last of the old Great Kings before the Concord's reorganization, and the first to be remembered as a tyrant rather than a founder.

## Tyranny

Where his ancestor [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Tuzlak the Great King\|Tuzlak the Great King]] had built the realm, the Big G intended only to hold it. His rule was marked by heavy taxation, a standing army of conscripts, and the steady silencing of the valley elders who remembered the old accord.

## Fall

His reign ended in the unrest that would ultimately give rise to the [[rosvania/Wiki/Factions & Organizations/Tuzlean Concord\|Tuzlean Concord]] and the Taner line, making the Big G the crooked bridge between the Age of Demigods and the Age of Concord.
