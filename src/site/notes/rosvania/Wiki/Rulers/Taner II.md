**Taner II** ("Taner the Steadfast") is the second Great King of the Tuzlean Concord, heir to the work of his father [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Taner\|Taner]]. His reign is remembered as the one that proved the Concord could outlast its founder.

## Keeping the Accord

Where his father had built the accord, Taner II defended it — against the ambitions of the valley lords who tested the new crown, and against the first cracks of dynastic ambition that would one day trouble his descendants.
