> *"A crown is a promise written in blood — and every promise, in time, is broken."*

**Tuzlak the Great King** ("the Great King", also the **Lord of the Whispering Sands**) is the legendary founder of the line that would one day rule the desert realm of **Tuzland**. A demigod of the Age Before Ages, he is the ancestor whose name became the land itself.

## Rise

Born into the myth-shrouded Age Before Ages, Tuzlak was one of the last great demigods to walk the mortal world. Where later kings claimed thrones, Tuzlak claimed a wasteland — and by his will turned the **Whispering Sands** into a realm men could call home.

## Legacy

- His line would come to be known as the genealogical root of the House of the Great Kings, and eventually as the **Tuzlean Concord**.
- His descendants include **Taner**, the builder who forged the Concord, and the great kings after him.
- Rulers after him would style themselves in his shadow — among them the infamous tyrant known only as **the Big G**.
