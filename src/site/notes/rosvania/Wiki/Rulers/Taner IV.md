**Taner IV** ("the Last Great King") is the final Great King of the Tuzlean Concord, heir to [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Taner III\|Taner III]] and the last of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Taner\|Taner]] line to wear the crown that [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Taner\|Taner the Builder]] first forged.

## The End of the Concord

His reign was the long twilight of the accord. The valley lords grew restless, the concord courts grew slow, and the bonds that had held Tuzland together for generations began to fray.

## Legacy

With Taner IV the great Tuzlean line ends. His name marks the boundary between the Second Age of concord and the Third Age, and the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Tuzland and the Whispering Sands\|Whispering Sands]] remember him as the last voice of the old accord.
