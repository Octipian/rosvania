**Taner III** ("Taner the Golden") is the third Great King of the Tuzlean Concord, ruling at the height of its prosperity and influence. His reign is the era remembered as the Concord's golden age.

## The Golden Reign

Under Taner III the Concord's caravans crossed the sands untroubled, its courts settled disputes fairly, and its name carried weight from the valley lords to the distant courts of the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Durakheim\|Durakheim]] and the [[rosvania/Wiki/Realms & Geography/Aurelian Dominion\|Aurelian Dominion]]. The peace he inherited, he left intact.

## Succession

He passed the crown to his son [[rosvania/Wiki/Rulers/Taner IV\|Taner IV]] — the last of the Concord's great kings, whose story would close the golden age.
